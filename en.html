What makes Damon braces good?

They are self-ligating braces: the archwire is held by a clip, not by ligatures. There is less friction, teeth move more gently and you need fewer visits to the orthodontist. They come in metal and ceramic — the ceramic ones are almost invisible.

How often should I see a dentist?

A check-up every 6 months, even if nothing bothers you. That way problems are found early, when treatment is simpler and cheaper.

How much does treatment cost?

The cost depends on the diagnosis and the materials you choose. The doctor gives you the plan and the cost before any work begins — no surprises at the end.

How long does an implant take to heal?

Usually 3 to 6 months. It depends on the jaw, the bone volume and your general health. The doctor will make an exact plan after an X-ray and a consultation.

Why a professional cleaninga cleaning if I brush at home?

A toothbrush doesn't remove tartar or plaque between the teeth. A professional cleaning lowers the risk of cavities and gum inflammation. We recommend one every six months.
